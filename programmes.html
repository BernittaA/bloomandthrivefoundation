OUR PROGRAMMES

Practical support, education and prevention.

Our programmes are designed to improve health knowledge, encourage prevention and support the dignity of girls and women.

01

Bloom Girls

Adolescent health education focused on menstrual health, puberty, personal hygiene, self-care and confidence building.

We aim to create safe spaces where girls can ask questions and receive accurate, age-appropriate health information.

02

Bloom Women

Women's health education covering reproductive health, anaemia awareness, breast health, wellbeing and preventive care.

03

Bloom Health Talks

Community-based health education sessions delivered by health professionals on relevant health topics affecting girls and women.

04

Bloom Screen

Community health screening activities including basic health checks, health awareness and appropriate referrals.

05

Bloom Care

Dignity support including sanitary pad distribution, hygiene packs and access to useful health resources.
