WHO WE ARE

About Bloom & Thrive Foundation

Bloom & Thrive Foundation is a women-led organisation committed to improving the health and wellbeing of girls and women.

We work through health education, community health talks, preventive health activities, screenings, referrals and community outreach.

Our goal is to create safe and supportive environments where girls and women can access reliable health information, ask questions and receive support without shame or fear.

Our Vision

A world where every girl and woman has access to health education, preventive care, and the dignity to thrive.

OUR MISSION

What we aim to achieve

Bloom & Thrive Foundation seeks to improve the health and wellbeing of girls and women by increasing access to health education, promoting preventive care, supporting dignity and encouraging stronger community participation in women's health.

🌸 MEET OUR FOUNDER

Bernitta Baaba Ampah

Founder & Registered Nurse

"As a nurse, I created Bloom & Thrive to bridge the gap between clinical care and community outreach, turning health insights into everyday empowerment for girls and women."

Driven by a passion for women's health, I launched this initiative to break the silence surrounding menstrual and reproductive wellness. By combining my clinical background with community action, I ensure our programmes deliver impactful, compassionate and stigma-free care.
