WHO WE ARE

Care that empowers.

The Bloom & Thrive Foundation is a women-led initiative committed to improving the health and wellbeing of girls and women through education, health talks, screenings, and community outreach[cite: 4].

Bloom exists to ensure that women are not only cared for, but empowered to thrive.

Our Vision

A world where every girl and woman has access to health education, preventive care, and the dignity to thrive.

🌸 MEET OUR FOUNDER

Bernitta Baaba Ampah[cite: 4]

Founder & Registered Nurse

"As a nurse, I created Bloom & Thrive to bridge the gap between clinical care and community outreach, turning health insights into everyday empowerment for girls and women."

Driven by a passion for women’s health, I launched this initiative to break the silence surrounding menstrual and reproductive wellness. By combining my clinical background with community action, I ensure our programmes deliver impactful, compassionate, and stigma-free care.
