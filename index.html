WHO WE ARE

Care that empowers.

Bloom & Thrive Foundation is a women-led organisation committed to improving the health and wellbeing of girls and women through education, health talks, screenings and community outreach.

We believe women should not only receive care, but should be empowered with the knowledge and support needed to thrive.

OUR PROGRAMMES

Practical support, education and prevention.

01

Bloom Girls

Adolescent health education including menstrual health, puberty and confidence building.

02

Bloom Women

Women's health education including reproductive health, anaemia awareness, breast health and wellbeing.

03

Bloom Health Talks

Community-based health education sessions led by women health professionals.

04

Bloom Screen

Basic health screening activities and appropriate health information and referrals.

05

Bloom Care

Dignity support including sanitary pad distribution, hygiene packs and health resources.
